About Karo Pitch

Built to unlock investor access for Bharat founders.

Thousands of founders are building amazing businesses but lack access to investors. Karo Pitch was created to bridge this gap with a structured platform where startups can apply, get shortlisted, pitch live, and build long-term investor relationships. Our mission is to support ambitious founders from every corner of Bharat with visibility, guidance, and fundraising opportunities.
